## 🚨 **Red Flags to Avoid**

**Not Every "Breakthrough" is Real:**

❌ **Avoid:**
- Press releases with no peer review
- "Revolutionary" claims from unknown companies
- Stocks with only promotional news
- Penny stocks with no real technology
- Chinese reverse mergers (often fraud)

✅ **Look For:**
- Peer-reviewed science
- Partnerships with major companies (Google, NASA, etc.)
- Government contracts (DOD, DOE)
- Institutional buying (check 13F filings)
- Real products/technology
